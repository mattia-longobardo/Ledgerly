## Two behaviours the plan text predates — do not describe the old ones

Both changed late in the phase. Any document that says otherwise is wrong.

1. **There is no automatic back-posting sweep.** `runInterestAccrualJob` posts the **current Rome-calendar day's accrual only**. A day that could not be posted is logged (`interest_post_skipped`) and counted so an operator is alerted; **nothing recovers it automatically**. The only in-app lever is re-triggering the daily job on the *same Rome-calendar day*, and it expires at the `romeDate` rollover. An earlier version swept up to 30 days of backlog; that would have back-posted the entire analyze-only parallel-run backlog the instant `postingMode` flipped — at exactly step 4 of the cut-over — with only an unverified Wallet filter semantic standing between that and duplicated real money. Ruling **P3-C37** removed it outright rather than bounding it, because bounding it correctly needs a "posting enabled at" fact the schema does not carry. See `src/lib/jobs/interest-accrual.ts:58-88` and `docs/deploy/phase-3-runbook.md` §5.
2. **`reconcileInterest` has an explicit `no_data` status.** `ReconciliationStatus` is `"matched" | "missing" | "delayed" | "anomalous" | "no_data"`. `no_data` means no accrual rows exist for the period — a missing basis for comparison, not evidence that nothing was owed. `missing` is narrower and different: accruals exist and nothing has been paid against them. Ruling **P3-C29 item 5**; threaded through the use cases, the API and both pages.

Two new vertical slices, both following the `modules/accounts` shape (ruling P3-2: flat `UseCaseDeps` bag, RLS context opened once by the caller).
